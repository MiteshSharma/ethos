Summary: the page is a regional grid reliability report for 2031.

Key points:
- Demand peaked in August at just over 41 gigawatts.
- The interconnect handled the peak without shedding any load, and
  reserves stayed above ten percent throughout.
- There were a handful of short curtailment events on the western spur.
- Figures come from settlement meters rather than real-time telemetry,
  and are revised before they are final.
- The study window contained almost no storage, so the conclusions do
  not carry over to a storage-heavy grid.
