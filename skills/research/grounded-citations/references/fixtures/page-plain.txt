# Regional Grid Reliability Report 2031

## Summary

Peak regional demand reached 41.2 gigawatts on 14 August 2031.

## Findings

The interconnect absorbed the surge without load shedding, and the
reserve margin never fell below eleven percent.

Operators logged three curtailment events, all on the western spur,
none lasting more than nine minutes.

## Method

Figures are drawn from the settlement meter archive, not from the
real-time telemetry feed. Settlement figures are revised twice before
they are final, so the values here may differ from the numbers
published in the operator's daily bulletin.

## Limitations

No storage assets larger than 20 megawatt-hours were in service during
the study window, so the findings do not generalise to a grid with
substantial storage.
